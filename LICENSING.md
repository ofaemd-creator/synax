The SYNAX (.sx) programming language, its compiler passes, runtime engine, and associated tooling are licensed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**.

## Why Apache 2.0?

1. **Permissive & Open:** You are free to use, modify, distribute, and embed SYNAX in both commercial and open-source projects without mandatory copyleft restrictions.
2. **Contributor Patent Protection:** Section 3 provides an express, royalty-free, perpetual patent grant from every contributor to all users.
3. **Trademark Protection:** Section 6 explicitly protects project names, logos, and trademarks (`SYNAX`, `SYNAXCODE`, `Synapse-Nano`), preventing confusing or misleading use.
4. **Standard in Compiler & Systems Infrastructure:** Major systems projects (including LLVM runtime components, Apache TVM, Android, Kubernetes) utilize Apache 2.0.
