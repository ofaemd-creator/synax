- This software must **NEVER** be used to direct patient care, defibrillation, cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), or any life-critical emergency medical decision.

## 2. Intended Academic Research Scope
Any cardiac rhythm analysis or signal quality assessment implemented within SYNAX is restricted to retrospective, non-interventional benchmark studies on open scientific databases (such as PhysioNet CU-DB, MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database). Outputs such as `SHOCKABLE_CANDIDATE` or `INDETERMINATE` represent computational classifications within research benchmarks and do not constitute medical opinions or treatment indications.
